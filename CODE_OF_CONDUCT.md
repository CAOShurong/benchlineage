# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We aim for a technical community where people can question assumptions, report errors, and discuss
measurement limitations without personal hostility.

Examples of constructive behavior include:

- distinguishing recorded evidence from inference;
- explaining disagreement with reproducible details;
- welcoming corrections from people with different levels of laboratory experience;
- protecting private, proprietary, and safety-sensitive data;
- giving credit for ideas and contributions.

Harassment, discrimination, deliberate deception, doxxing, and publication of private laboratory
records are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Maintainers may
edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.
