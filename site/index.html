THE EVIDENCE CHAIN

A plot is not a provenance record.

BenchLineage keeps the decisions and identities around a measurement visible. Every layer is a plain file that can be reviewed, versioned, migrated, and audited.

01 / INTENT

Study

Objective, hypothesis, protocol, and tags preserve why the experiment exists.

02 / TRACE

Instrument

Manufacturer, model, serial, asset tag, and calibration window establish recorded identity.

03 / OBSERVE

Run

Operator, timestamp, conditions, deviations, and raw paths connect intent to observations.

04 / DERIVE

Analysis

Deterministic results name their source run and keep uncertainty assumptions explicit.

05 / AUDIT

Relationships

Cross-file checks expose missing references, calibration gaps, orphan data, and path escape.

06 / SEAL

Evidence root

SHA-256 inventories detect byte changes while keeping the integrity claim deliberately narrow.

HONEST CLAIMS

Integrity is one part of trustworthy measurement.

A digest can show that bytes match. It cannot turn weak experimental design into strong evidence.

Not an electronic signature.

A local seal does not establish identity, authorship, or a trusted timestamp.

Not calibration authority.

Certificate references and coverage dates are recorded, not independently authenticated.

Not regulatory compliance.

Permissions, validated workflows, retention, and institutional governance require other systems.

Not scientific truth.

Fabricated data can still be internally consistent. Review methods, raw observations, and uncertainty assumptions.

PORTABLE BY DEFAULT
